Our commitments

Your videos hold personal information about you and the people who appear in them. That’s why we’ve set clear rules for how we handle them, and we make those rules public.

Learn more
We never use your videos to train AI

We don’t use your videos, voice, or text to train AI, and we don’t use them for advertising. When outside providers handle AI processing for us, we send your data only under terms and settings that keep it out of model training.

We ask for your consent before sending anything

Before any video is sent to an outside AI service, we ask for your consent in the app. You can withdraw it at any time, and features that run entirely on your device will keep working.

We delete your data on a set schedule

When you delete a project, its data is deleted from our servers too. Anything left behind is deleted automatically 30 days after it was last processed. You keep all rights to the videos you create.
