1. Business Operator and Scope

This Policy sets out how ChromaGrad AI, LLC ("we," "us" or "our") handles your personal information and other information in the app "Shoot Assist" (Japanese name: 撮影アシスト) that we provide and in related services (collectively, the "Service").

Although we are a business located in the United States, we provide the Service to people living in Japan, and we therefore handle information in compliance with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (the "APPI").

In this Policy, "personal information" means personal information as defined in the APPI. Faces captured in videos and recorded voices are also personal information if they make it possible to identify a specific individual.

3. Information Processed Only on Your Device

The following processing takes place only on your device, and its results are not sent to our server (although counts of events and similar data may be sent as usage data; see Section 2). However, features that use external AI send the videos themselves (see Section 5).

  • Speech recognition for script guidance during shooting and for subtitles when you finish a video on your device: only the speech recognition built into your device is used, and nothing is sent to Apple's servers or to our server. Transcription for AI editing and on-screen text is a separate process, performed by sending audio to external AI providers (see Section 5).

  • Face position detection: used for framing guidance and for mosaics that hide faces. We do not perform any processing that identifies individuals by their faces (face authentication or facial recognition).

  • Detection of people, hands, objects and animals; depth estimation; measurement of camera shake, tilt and brightness; beat detection; and spatial understanding through AR.

  • Location coordinates (latitude and longitude): stored on your device. Place names converted from them (e.g., "Shibuya") may be sent to our server as clip tags. For conversion to place names and related matters, see Section 10.

  • Projects, clips, chat history, AD profile and settings: stored on your device. Only what is needed is sent to our server, at the time you use the relevant feature. For post idea suggestions, this information is sent automatically once a day when you open the Home screen.

4. Purposes of Use

We use the information we collect only for the following purposes.

  1. Providing the Service (shooting guidance, tips while you shoot, clip tagging, highlight extraction, editing, creating on-screen text, chat replies, and post idea suggestions)

  2. Making suggestions tailored to you based on what the AI has remembered from your chats and on your AD profile

  3. Responding to outages, investigating bugs, and improving the Service by analyzing usage data

  4. Preventing abuse and ensuring safety

  5. Managing AI usage and costs (including totaling costs per device)

  6. Responding to inquiries

  7. Sending important notices, such as changes to our terms

  8. Responding as required by laws and regulations

We do not use the videos, audio or text you send for advertising or for training AI models. If we change the purposes of use, we will follow the procedure in Section 15.

5. Entrustment to External Providers, Including AI Providers

We entrust the handling of information to the following providers. We send each provider only the information necessary for its processing.

Data flow: your device sends videos, audio and chats to our server (Railway, California, USA), which sends them to Google (Gemini API) and ElevenLabs (USA); your device itself sends data directly only to Apple and Japan's Geospatial Information Authority (see Section 10).

The only parties other than us to which your device sends information directly are Apple and the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (see Section 10).

Each provider handles information as follows.

  • Google: We use the Gemini API under a paid plan, and Google does not use the information sent to it to improve its products or to train AI. Google logs information for up to 55 days to detect abuse, and if a problem is suspected, Google personnel may review it. If "Web search" is used, search-related information is retained for 30 days under Google's terms. For some features, video files are kept on Google's servers for up to 48 hours. Google handles information within the scope of our instructions, based on its data processing terms.

  • ElevenLabs: We have turned off the setting that allows the data we send to be used to improve ElevenLabs' AI. However, ElevenLabs' terms do not make clear whether this setting also applies to audio sent through the API. ElevenLabs retains transcription results in accordance with its own terms. ElevenLabs determines the retention period, and we cannot specify it. Data remains in backups for up to 30 days.

  • Railway: Our server and storage are located in Railway's facilities in the United States (California). Railway handles information only within the scope of our instructions.

We require the providers to which we send information to protect it to the same standard as this Policy, and we confirm this through data processing agreements and each provider's terms. Handling specific to each provider is as described above.

Before sending videos and images, we remove location information such as where they were shot (metadata). Videos used for editing are downscaled to about 480 pixels wide, and reference videos to about 720 pixels. If the original video is smaller than that, it is sent as is. Images are converted to JPEGs no more than 640 pixels wide before they are sent.

If you provide a link in chat, Google (Gemini API) reads the content of the linked page. Our server does not retrieve videos from links.

11. Children's Information

  1. The Service is not intended for people under 18, and people under 18 may not use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from people under 18.

  2. If we learn that a person under 18 has been using the Service, we will promptly delete the information we received from that person. Parents or guardians who become aware of this should contact the contact point in Section 16.

  3. If a video you shoot mainly shows a person under 18, please use the Service only after obtaining the consent of that person's parent or guardian.

12. Requests for Disclosure, Correction, Suspension of Use, etc., and Procedures

  1. Under the APPI, you may request, with respect to your personal data that we hold, notification of the purposes of use, disclosure (including records of provision to third parties), correction, addition or deletion, suspension of use or erasure, and suspension of provision to third parties.

  2. Please send requests by email to the contact point in Section 16. When doing so, please tell us the "Support ID" shown in the app's settings. We use this ID to locate the relevant data and also to verify your identity. For requests made through an agent, we also verify the agent's authority. We will respond without delay and make disclosures by whichever method you prefer, electronic data or a paper document. We do not charge a fee.

  3. Because the Service has no accounts, we do not manage data on our server by linking it to names or similar information. If you do not know your Support ID, we may be unable to identify the relevant data. In that case, we will let you know.

  4. You can check, change and delete information on your device yourself in the app. You can delete what AD has remembered on the Profile screen, one item at a time or all at once. You can also set AD not to remember things. If you turn off "Share usage data" on the Profile screen, usage data stops being sent, and the data accumulated on your device is also erased.

  5. You can withdraw your consent to the use of external AI at any time in the app's settings. After you withdraw it, we will not send information to external AI. Data already on our server is deleted within the periods in Section 8. If you want it deleted immediately, please contact the contact point in Section 16.

  6. Where laws and regulations provide that we need not comply with a request, we may decline it, stating the reason.
